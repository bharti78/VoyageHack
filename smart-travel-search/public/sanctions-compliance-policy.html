Sanctions Compliance
Policy

Definitions

\"Company\" or \"TBO\" shall mean TBO Tek Limited and its subsidiaries.

\"HMT\" means Her Majesty's Treasury.

\"OFAC\" means Office of Foreign Assets Control, United States Department of Treasury.

\"Restricted Party\" means a person listed, owned or controlled by a sanctioned person, or acting on behalf of one.

\"Sanctioned Country\" means a country or territory targeted by comprehensive sanctions from time to time.

\"Sanctioned Person\" means any individual or entity that is subject to sectoral sanctions, prohibitions, or restrictions under applicable sanctions laws.

\"Sanctions\" means laws, regulations, embargoes or restrictive measures administered, enacted or enforced by any Sanctions Authority.

1. Policy Statement

TBO is fully committed to conducting its business in compliance with all laws and trade restrictions imposed by applicable national and supranational authorities.

TBO values honest and respectful business conduct and means that compliance is an integral part of TBO's future success.

2. Restrictions on Business

  1. TBO shall not engage in any transaction or business with a Restricted Party or in breach of Sanctions.
  2. All officers, directors, employees and agents of TBO shall conduct business activities in compliance with Sanctions.
  3. TBO shall not directly or indirectly provide funds, credit, trade, or services that violate applicable Sanctions.
  4. TBO officers, directors, employees, agents, or representatives shall not cause, facilitate, or participate in prohibited transactions.

3. Potential Impact of Sanctions Violations

  1. Sanctions violations may result in criminal and civil penalties and significant reputational damage.
  2. Violations by one country may have cross-border impact on individuals and companies.
  3. Any violation may severely damage banking relationships and TBO's ability to conduct routine transactions.

4. Responsibilities of Employees

  1. All employees involved in international transactions must understand and follow this policy.
  2. Keep up to date with laws and regulations applicable to the countries in which you operate.
  3. Perform customer due diligence and know your customers, their business, and intended use of services.
  4. Seek advice from your manager or ethics officer if you have any doubt about a transaction.
